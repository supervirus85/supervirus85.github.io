Why micro-influencers

Smaller audiences. Bigger trust.

Micro-influencers typically have a few thousand to around a hundred thousand followers — and audiences that genuinely listen to them.

  • Closer, more trusted relationships with followers
  • Higher engagement than many celebrity accounts
  • Affordable for startups and small businesses
  • Niche communities for precise targeting
  • Authentic content that feels like a recommendation, not an ad
